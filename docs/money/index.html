How to read the crowding band. Asset classes: where today's SHARE sits versus its own history. Commodities: where the speculator-minus-hedger positioning gap sits versus its own history (CFTC data). ● green = typical (25th-75th percentile), ■ yellow = elevated (10-25th / 75-90th), ▲ red = extreme (top or bottom 10%). Extremes are a contrarian warning, not a timing signal.

Limits. Shares move with prices, not only with money flows. Source data is quarterly or monthly, so "current" means the latest reading available.
